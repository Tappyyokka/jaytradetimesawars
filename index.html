About the Program

Recognising the People Behind Success

The JAY TRADE VYAPARI VYAVASAYI MSME AWARDS is an initiative dedicated to recognizing, appreciating, and uplifting small-scale business owners, traders, entrepreneurs, and Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) who form the backbone of our economy.

The awards aspire to create a supportive business ecosystem where small-scale entrepreneurs can connect, grow, learn, and expand their ventures. By recognizing excellence, promoting local businesses, and encouraging sustainable business practices, the initiative seeks to inspire the next generation of entrepreneurs and strengthen the MSME sector.
